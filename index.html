Creator’s Notes

Hello, my name is Bentley.

I created this when I was 15. I had been struggling with my mental health for a long time and never felt comfortable opening up to the people around me. Talking about the heavier things always felt impossible, so I decided to build a place where those thoughts could exist without an audience.

I’m still struggling with my mental health. Some days are heavier than others, and I don’t have it all figured out. This page is something I made in the middle of that, not after it was over.

This site is completely private. Nothing you write is uploaded or shared. Everything stays only on your device. I made it this way on purpose — so it could feel safe.

I’m really proud of this page. It means more to me than I can fully explain. It started as something small I made for myself, and watching it become a space that others might also find useful has been one of the few things that’s made me feel like I actually created something that matters.

You can write the short, sharp things that sit in your chest, and you can also choose to release them when you’re ready. Holding on is allowed. Letting go is allowed too.

I don’t claim this is a solution. It’s just a quiet corner of the internet I wish I had when I was younger. A place to be honest without having to perform strength for anyone else.

If you are in crisis, please reach out for real support. In the United States you can call or text 988. You deserve help that goes beyond a webpage.

Thank you for being here.
